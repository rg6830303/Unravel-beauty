# Unravel Beauty Secrets — Storefront Redesign ("Botanical Atelier")

> Live Storefront: **https://rg6830303.github.io/Unravel-beauty/**

High-performance, elegant storefront redesign for [Unravel Beauty Secrets](https://unravelbeautysecrets.in/), an Indian Ayurvedic beauty brand. Formulated with authentic Ayurvedic roots and pure botanicals in Kolkata, India.

---

## 🌿 Design System: "Botanical Atelier"
* **Warm Ivory**: `#F7F3EB`
* **Deep Aubergine**: `#432735`
* **Muted Saffron**: `#C79958`
* **Pale Blush**: `#E9DADA`
* **Sage**: `#DDE1D5`
* **Typography**: Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display`, `Cinzel`) paired with geometric functional UI sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

---

## ✨ Features & Architecture
* **Instant Live Search**: Real-time query matching across products, botanicals, and skin concerns with instant "Add to Bag" action, recent searches, and popular tags.
* **Silky Smooth Motion Graphics**: GPU-accelerated page transitions, ambient floating saffron & herbal botanicals, interactive button micro-interactions, and progress bar shimmer.
* **Full E-Commerce Flow**:
  * Shopping bag & sliding mini-bag drawer with live count badges.
  * Free shipping threshold calculator (Free above ₹1,999; ₹50 prepaid / ₹100 COD).
  * 1-click wishlist toggle with count badge.
  * Product detail pages with dynamic size variant selectors (110 ml vs 220 ml).
  * AM / PM daily routine builder.
  * Guest-first checkout flow with address validation and Razorpay / COD payment modes.
  * Order confirmation receipt with Delhivery dispatch tracking.
  * Masterclass soap-making workshop waitlist registration.
  * Customer account dashboard with mock order history.
  * Full policy suite: Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and Delivery & Returns.
